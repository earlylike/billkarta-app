# BillKarta Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

BillKarta is an app for recording work duties and creating bills.

**Data we collect:** None. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics, and we do not
collect, sell or share your personal data.

**Data stored on your phone:** The names, locations, dates, rates, advances and phone
numbers you enter are stored only inside the app on your device. We cannot see them.
Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them.

**Files you create:** PDF bills and backup files are saved to your Downloads/BillKarta
folder on your request. You control these files.

**WhatsApp:** If you tap the WhatsApp button, the bill text is passed to the WhatsApp app
on your phone. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies from there.

**Permissions:** The app requests no permissions and does not itself connect to the internet.

**Children:** The app is not directed at children.
